# Regretcast — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Group workforce strategy lead

- As a workforce strategy lead, I want to register a commitment and score its payoff in all four worlds in a single sitting, so that multi-scenario discipline costs minutes rather than a workshop and therefore actually gets done.
- As a workforce strategy lead, I want the portfolio split between no-regrets moves and bets with committed spend against each, so that I can tell the executive how much of the plan depends on one future being right.
- As a workforce strategy lead, I want to compare two competing plans on regret exposure rather than on expected value alone, so that a plan which is slightly worse on average but survives all four worlds can win on its merits.

### Country HR director

- As a country HR director, I want to see which group commitments land in my jurisdiction and the consultation and notice timetable each one implies, so that group lead times are built on my legal reality rather than on a group-average assumption.
- As a country HR director, I want to reject a commitment as not executable in my jurisdiction inside its assumed lead time, with my reasons attached to the commitment, so that an undeliverable plan is corrected at the register rather than discovered at implementation.
- As a country HR director, I want to see the in-country redeployment options for affected roles before a reduction is proposed, so that we can evidence that suitable alternative employment was considered in the right order.

### Finance business partner

- As a finance business partner, I want every commitment tied to a budget line with its stranded-cost exposure per world, so that the workforce plan and the financial plan cannot disagree quietly.
- As a finance business partner, I want to see which bets have tripwire reviews falling due before the next capital approval, so that we do not approve spend on a bet whose premise is already under review.
- As a finance business partner, I want reskilling commitments re-forecast against actual take-up, so that a programme assuming enthusiastic voluntary participation is corrected in the numbers when participation does not arrive.

### Employee representative

- As a works council representative, I want the information and consultation pack for a commitment delivered at the point the duty is triggered, including the assumptions underlying it, so that consultation is genuine rather than a notification of a settled decision.
- As an employee representative, I want to see the aggregate sentiment reading that management is reporting to the board, so that the workforce's own account of its anxiety is part of the same evidence base as the plan.
- As an employee representative, I want to see how many commitments were classified as bets in my country and what tripwires govern them, so that I can raise consequences for members before a threshold fires.

### Chief People Officer and governance administrator

- As a Chief People Officer, I want a scheduled portfolio review showing regret exposure, tripwire status and a world-drift assessment, timed to land before capital and headcount approvals, so that the board decides on the portfolio rather than on individual business cases.
- As a Chief People Officer, I want to be alerted when a bet's tripwire has fired and the pre-agreed response has not been executed within its window, so that the failure mode the whole design exists to prevent does not recur silently.
- As a governance administrator, I want to manage confidentiality tiers and inspect the disclosure log for any reduction scenario, so that I can answer who knew about a site closure scenario, and when, if that question is ever asked by a regulator, a court or a works council.
- As a governance administrator, I want world definitions and payoff rubrics versioned with authorship and locked against retrospective edits, so that a past decision is judged against the beliefs that produced it.
